## 🧩 About

**Knight Online Grind** is a comprehensive overlay menu for Knight Online featuring auto-farm loops, EXP and drop trackers, loot radar, inventory HUD, session timers, and grind route helpers.

Based on popular overlay tools like **KO Helper**, **GrindKit**, and **MMO Overlay**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Grind Tools
- Auto-Farm Loop — repeat skill and attack rotations on a timer
- EXP Tracker — live EXP/hour and time-to-level estimates
- Loot Radar — highlight nearby drops and chests
- Inventory HUD — weight, slots, and durability at a glance
- Session Timer — track grind sessions and break reminders

### 🗺️ Map & Route Helpers
- Grind Route Overlay — waypoints for popular farming zones
- Mob Density Map — visualize crowded spawn areas
- Respawn Timer — predict rare spawn windows
- Party Sync — share markers with co-op teammates

### 🏋️ Quality of Life
- Auto-Repair Reminder — alert before gear breaks
- Potion Tracker — monitor consumable counts
- Chat Filter — hide spam during long sessions
- Hotkey Profiles — swap layouts per character
